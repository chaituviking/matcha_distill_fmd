
The assignment says mentioned on CosyVoice 2 so i tried it as a second teacher, not to replace matcha but to actually measure the cost of using it instead of just saying its too
much. It loads and synthesizes fine, the catch is the flow decoder isnt standalone, it needs conditioning that the LM stage produces, so i ran a normal zero-shot synthesis
once and hooked the decoder to grab and cache what it receives, then i never touch the LM again.

The real wall is the channels. their estimator wants 320 because it packs x and mu and spks and cond, my student only sends x and mu so 160, it fails at the first conv. so
this isnt a teacher swap, its changes to the student and the loss which are the frozen files, and that is exactly the extraction cost the readme talks about, now a number
not a claim. measured it anyway while i was there, 71M params vs matcha 11M, roughly 40 times times more per NFE, decoder RTF 0.74 at NFE 32 so slower than real time on cpu.

one honest thing, i dont report cosyvoice quality per NFE. my decode there is a simple euler loop just to time the estimator, its not their real sampler (CFG, cosine schedule,
causal cache), so the audio is not representative, a quick check gave WER nearly 0.9 even at NFE 32 which is a sampler artefact not the model. cost timing is faithful, quality via
that path isnt.

so matcha stays the teacher for the distillation, cosyvoice stays as the cost reference for what production actually looks like. next time on a gpu i extend the student to take
spks and cond so the 320 line up and then it can really be distilled.
